# CalmCarry Companion App - v1 Build Scope
**Task Force AI Dev. Services L.L.C. × Glowco · $10,000 fixed (first-engagement rate)**

Stack: React Native (Expo) · NestJS + PostgreSQL · Shopify bundle entitlement · headless CMS · audio CDN.
Principle: ship a **complete, on-brand, store-ready v1.** The heavy/risky modules are phased to a paid Phase 2 - that protects the $10k from becoming a death-march *and* creates the future work.

## ✅ In scope - what we build for $10k
- **Premium, on-brand design + a reusable design system** ← the #1 priority. Locked before any screens are built.
- Cross-platform app (iOS + Android, one Expo codebase).
- Auth + Shopify/Glowco identity login.
- **Free tier:** serial/warranty registration, authenticity check, replacement-claim form, 20-minute wind-down timer, a few sample tracks.
- **Paid tier (bundle-unlock, no in-app purchase):** streaming content library (from the CMS) + 1–2 guided programs, unlocked by the Shopify bundle order.
- **Backend:** auth, entitlement service, warranty, signed-URL content delivery, Shopify order-webhook sync.
- App Store + Google Play submission.

## 🔜 Phase 2 - NOT in the $10k (future paid work)
- Offline encrypted downloads + sync (the heaviest engineering).
- Family & kids mode + COPPA (legal + engineering workstream).
- Multi-week structured-program engine + advanced tracking/insight.
- BLE device control (if a future device adds Bluetooth) · subscription tier.

## 🚫 Excluded - Glowco provides
- **All audio/media content** (scripts, narration, production).
- Shopify Plus access + API, app-store developer accounts, production hosting/CMS/CDN subscriptions, **brand assets**.
- Legal/compliance sign-off (we build the guardrails; Glowco owns the medical-claim / PDPL / COPPA legal).

## Definition of done
Approved and **live on both app stores**, against the in-scope list. Post-launch support = a separate retainer, not part of the $10k.

- A **real, version-controlled repo** - Expo app + NestJS backend - not a throwaway vibe-build.
- **AI-accelerated, senior-verified:** AI does the scaffolding/boilerplate; human judgment owns design, architecture, auth/entitlement, and app-store. The hard 10% gets done, not skipped.
