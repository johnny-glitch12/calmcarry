# CalmCarry — App Store Privacy Disclosures

> **Scope note:** Every answer below is derived strictly from CalmCarry's stated data-collection facts. CalmCarry does **no** third-party tracking, runs **no** ads, and **does not sell or share data for cross-app advertising**. All subscriptions are in-app purchases processed by Apple/Google; CalmCarry never receives or stores payment card data.

## 2. Google Play — Data Safety

| Data Type | Collected? | Shared? | Purpose | Optional? |

### Security practices

- **Is all data encrypted in transit?** **Yes.** Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
- **Data sold to third parties?** **No.**
- **Data shared with third parties?** **No.**
- **Ads / third-party advertising?** **None.**

### Data deletion

- **Can users request that data be deleted?** **Yes.**
- **Deletion path:** Users can delete their account and all associated data **in-app** (Settings → Account → Delete Account), and may also request deletion by contacting The Glow Company at the support address listed on the store page. Account deletion removes identity, household profiles, listen logs, push token, and any device-registration data.
- *Note:* Subscription billing records held by Apple/Google are managed by those stores, not by CalmCarry.

## 3. In-App Privacy Summary (plain English)

**Your privacy, in plain words.**

CalmCarry is built to help you wind down — not to watch you. We only collect what we need to run your account and make the app work for you:

- **Your account** — the email and name from your Sign in with Apple, Google, or email.
- **Your household** — the profiles and names you choose for the adults and children in your home.
- **Your sessions** — light notes of which sessions you've listened to, so you can pick up where you left off. We do **not** track moods, symptoms, or any health scores.
- **Reminders** — if you turn them on, a notification token so we can send the gentle nudges you asked for.
- **Your device** — if you choose to register your CalmCarry, its serial number for warranty.

**What we never do:** We don't track you across other apps or websites. We don't show ads. We don't sell or share your data with anyone. Subscriptions are handled securely by Apple or Google — we never see your card details. Your data is encrypted on its way to us, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Settings.

The CalmCarry device works perfectly on its own. The app is an optional way to go deeper — and your trust comes first.

## 4. COPPA / Kids-Data Note

CalmCarry is built **adult-first**, with an optional, parent-managed **Kids Mode** designed to align with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) ahead of the **April 2026** compliance deadline.

- **Parent-gated by design.** Children's profiles are created and managed only by an adult account holder behind a **parent-gated PIN**. Children do not create their own accounts.
- **No child sign-in / no child contact data.** We do not knowingly collect a child's email, phone number, or other personal contact information. A "child profile" is only an adult-chosen label and name attached to the parent's account.
- **No behavioral tracking of children.** No advertising, no ad identifiers, and no third-party tracking are present anywhere in the app, including Kids Mode.
- **No third-party sharing.** Children's profile data is never sold or shared with third parties.
- **No social surface for kids.** The anonymous community is **adults-only**; Kids Mode contains **zero chat** and no user-to-user communication.
- **Parental control.** The parent can view and delete a child's profile and associated data at any time via account settings; deleting the account removes all child-profile data.
- **Verifiable parental involvement.** Because all child profiles live under a verified adult account and are gated behind a parental PIN, collection of any child-associated data occurs only with parental action and consent.

> **Wellness, not medical.** CalmCarry is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Kids Mode is a calming, wind-down experience only.
