# Why CalmCarry Wins

*A one-pager for the team. CalmCarry is the official app companion to The Glow Company's handheld CalmCarry device.*

---

## The Wedge

Every serious competitor is an **app, and only an app**. We start with something they can't copy overnight: **a real object you hold in your hand.**

- **A physical companion in an app-only category.** Calm, Headspace, and BetterSleep are screens. CalmCarry begins with a handheld you rest in your palm that delivers a gentle pulsing or tingling sensation, set to a comfortable level. The app is the optional layer that makes it richer - not the whole product.
- **The device works perfectly on its own.** There's no setup wall, no "create an account to begin." You can have a calmer moment the second the device arrives. The app is an optional way to go deeper, never a gate. (The app does not connect to or control the device - you use it while holding it, following gentle on-screen cues.)
- **Honest billing vs. the category's #1 trust-killer.** The loudest complaint about sleep apps is surprise charges and buried cancel buttons. We do the opposite: a clear pre-charge reminder before any renewal, one-tap cancel, no dark patterns, and **no free-trial trap** - the free tier is a genuine taste, not a countdown to a charge.
- **Calmer and less cluttered.** Rivals pile on streaks, badges, leaderboards, and noisy upsells - the exact opposite of winding down. CalmCarry is quiet by design: fewer choices, softer surfaces, nothing that makes a busy mind busier.
- **A genuinely safe kids mode.** Parent-gated PIN, COPPA-aligned, zero chat, no ads, no third-party sharing. A space parents can hand to a child without a second thought - something the optimization-first incumbents have never prioritized.

**One line:** *The device gives you relief on day one. The app is how you go deeper - gently, honestly, on your terms.*

## North-Star Metric

**The first completed device-in-hand session, then day-30 retention.**

1. **Activation - first completed session with the device in hand.** Not a signup, not an app open - the moment someone finishes a guided session while holding the device and feels the difference. This is where the product's promise becomes real, and it's the single best predictor of everything downstream.
2. **Day-30 retention.** Did the calmer moment become a habit? Thirty-day retention tells us we delivered real relief, earned trust (honest billing matters here), and gave people a reason to keep coming back - the foundation for sustainable subscription revenue.

**Why this pair:** activation proves the wedge works for a new person; day-30 retention proves it keeps working. Optimize the journey from *device arrives* to *first completed session in hand* to *still here on day 30*, and the freemium-to-premium engine takes care of itself.
