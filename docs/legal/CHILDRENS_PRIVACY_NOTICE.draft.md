## In one paragraph

CalmCarry has a kids mode. A parent creates it, a parent controls it, and it is deliberately built so that we do not receive information about your child. The only thing kids mode asks for is a first name, and that name stays on your phone. We never upload it. While a child profile is active, we collect no usage analytics, we send no crash reports, we show no advertising, and there is nothing your child can post, share, or send to another person.

## What kids mode collects

**A first name. That is the entire list.**

When you create a child profile, the app asks you to type a first name so the app can greet your child. That name is stored in the app's storage on your own device. It is never sent to our servers. It is not backed up to our systems. We cannot see it, we cannot search it, and we could not produce it if we were asked to.

Kids mode does not ask for and does not collect:

- last name
- date of birth or age
- email address, phone number, or any other contact detail
- home address or any location information
- photographs, video, or audio recordings
- voice recordings or voiceprints, or any other biometric information
- contacts, calendar, or any other data from your phone
- any government identifier

## What kids mode does NOT do

**No usage analytics.** CalmCarry records some anonymous product analytics on adult profiles. While a child profile is the active profile, that recording is switched off in code. Not sampled down, not anonymised further. Off.

**No crash or error reporting.** CalmCarry uses a crash reporting service on adult profiles to find bugs. When a child profile becomes active, that service is shut down entirely, and any event it would otherwise send is discarded before it leaves the phone.

**No advertising, of any kind.** No ads, no ad networks, no ad SDKs. We do not use the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not track your child across other apps or websites. We do not build a profile of your child. We do not sell, rent, or share your child's information, because we do not have it.

**No social features.** Kids mode has no chat, no messaging, no comments, no sharing, no leaderboards, no friend lists, and no public profile. There is no way for your child to communicate with anyone, and no way for anyone to contact your child through CalmCarry.

## The parent gate

Kids mode is protected by a four-digit PIN that you set, and optionally by Face ID. The PIN is stored on your device as a salted hash, which means the app can check whether a PIN is correct without ever storing the PIN itself. The gate is what stops a child leaving kids mode or reaching the adult areas of the app.

**Please read this part carefully.** The parent gate is a security control. It is not age verification, and it is not the same thing as verifiable parental consent under children's privacy law. We say this plainly because some apps blur the two, and we would rather you knew exactly what you have.

## How long we keep information

**Your child's first name:** it is on your device, so it lasts exactly as long as you keep it. Delete the child profile, or delete the app, and it is gone. We hold no copy to delete because we never received one.

**Adult account information:** session records and anonymous product analytics from adult profiles are automatically deleted 400 days after they are created, by an automated purge that runs every day.

## Your rights as a parent

Because we do not hold your child's information, most of these rights you can exercise yourself, immediately, without contacting us:

- **See what we have about your child.** Nothing. The first name is on your device and you can see it in the app at any time.
- **Delete it.** Delete the child profile in the app, or delete the app. That removes it.
- **Refuse to let us collect any more.** There is nothing to switch off, because there is nothing being collected. If that ever changes, we will tell you before it changes and ask you again.
- **Withdraw your acknowledgement.** You can remove the child profile at any time, for any reason, without affecting your adult account.

If you want any of this in writing from us, or you believe we hold something we should not, email Admin@glowco.co and we will respond within 30 days.

## App store categories

CalmCarry is not enrolled in Apple's Kids Category or Google Play's Designed for Families programme. That is a deliberate choice. The app is bought and set up by adults, and the kids mode sits inside an adult account behind a parent gate. Not being in those programmes does not reduce our obligations under children's privacy law, and it does not change anything described in this notice.
